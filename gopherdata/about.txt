About Gopher Protocol
======================

The Gopher protocol was designed to distribute, search, and
retrieve documents over the Internet. It was superseded by
the World Wide Web in the mid-1990s.

Key Features:
- Simple menu-based navigation
- Text-focused content delivery
- Lightweight protocol
- Hierarchical organization

RFC 1436 defines the Gopher protocol specification.

This server implements the core functionality of the Gopher
protocol, allowing you to serve files and directories through
the classic Gopher interface.
